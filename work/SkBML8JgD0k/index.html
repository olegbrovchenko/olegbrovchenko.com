POLARIS (Remix)

SAIKO, Feid, Quevedo ft. Mora

Directed by Oleg BrovchenkoReleased 2023-06-08Views 89M on YouTube, September 2026Watch on YouTube →

The SAKURA-era remix that put four of the biggest names in Spanish-language urban music in one frame. Oleg led the audiovisual production of the SAKURA rollout in 2024.

Oleg Brovchenko is a filmmaker and director based in Madrid. Self-taught since 2011, he has more than 130 music video credits with a combined 620 million views, and founded Full Drill Production in 2024.
